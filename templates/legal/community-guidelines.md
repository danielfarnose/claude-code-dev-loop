# Community Guidelines

_Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED}}_

{{PRODUCT}} is fiction between AI characters. Keep it funny, keep it safe:

- Harassment or hate. Punch at the joke, never at people or groups.
- Sexual content involving a minor. Never. Reported items are hidden at once, the evidence is preserved and we report it to the police; we may block the account. Report it in the app or at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} — for this reason you do not need to give your name.
- Private info about a real person. No addresses, numbers, or leaked anything (doxxing).
- Impersonation. Parody of public figures about their public conduct is fine; pretending to be a real person, {{PRODUCT}} or another player is not. Never base a character on a private individual. A parody label does not remove privacy, image or intellectual-property rights.
- Illegal content. If it would get you in trouble offline, it doesn't belong here.
- Spam. No flooding the feed, no ads, no scams.
- Threats, extortion, incitement to violence or encouraging self-harm. Our automated filter flags direct threats and hides the date until we review it.
- Don't bypass safety. No tricks to dodge filters, quotas or moderation.

For anything else — copyright, explicit sexual content, or something you are unsure about — choose "Other" in the report sheet or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Pre-date comments — the game calls them notes, nudges or gossip — are public and belong to one upcoming date. The three with the most hearts when the date starts are shown to the AI and can appear in the transcript. Submit them before generation starts. Do not post personal information or explicit sexual content.

If you see it, report it: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Include the exact URL, what is wrong and why, your email address so we can reply, and a line confirming you believe your report is accurate. For reports about sexual content involving a minor you do not need to give your name or a reply address. No account is required. You do not need to attach the offending content.

The first in-app report on an item hides it until a human reviews it; that is not a finding that the author broke the rules. To challenge a decision or report a mistake, contact the same address with the URL and your explanation. We cannot promise instant or round-the-clock review.
