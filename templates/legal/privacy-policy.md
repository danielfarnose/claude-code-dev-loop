## Your browser and your own API key

Game state is stored in localStorage. A key you provide is stored in sessionStorage, rather than the persisted game state, and is cleared by our sign-out and account-deletion flows. Browser session restoration may preserve sessionStorage: on a shared device, sign out and clear site data when finished.

Your own AI provider key never reaches us: the browser sends it directly to {{AI_PROVIDER}}, OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini, according to the provider selected. Prompts go to that provider under its terms; it may charge your account. A resulting conversation can still be published to {{DB_PROVIDER}}.

## What becomes public

Character names and appearances, scheduled pairings and places, pre-date comments under an alias, finished conversations, verdicts and reactions may be public. When you press SEND or finish a date, it is published automatically — there is no preview or approval step. Public links can be shared and copied by anyone.

If you set a nickname in Profile, it replaces the guest alias on your comments — the ones you write from then on and the ones you already wrote; transcripts already played keep their text as it was. Nicknames are public and moderated like any other content.

We do not show a character's full personality text on public pages. But the AI reads it to write the date, so the public dialogue may repeat what you wrote. If you share a custom character's link, that link contains the full personality text. For scheduled dates the server keeps the personality until the date is played. Never put private or sensitive information about yourself or anyone else into a character or a comment.

We do not ask for, and do not want, information about your health, sexual orientation, beliefs, ethnicity or similar. Everything you type into a character or comment is treated as fiction about that character; we do not use it to infer anything about you. If you have entered such information about yourself or someone else, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will remove it.

## Comments and news

Pre-date comments — the game calls them notes, nudges or gossip — are public and belong to one upcoming date. The three with the most hearts when the date starts are shown to the AI and can appear in the transcript. They are not carried into a repeat or an unrelated date. Comments close when generation starts.

The game also uses a shared feed of news-inspired situations built from public news sources. That feed is separate from player comments and contains no player data. News-inspired dialogue remains generated fiction and may be inaccurate.

## Artificial intelligence in the game

AI models generate fictional dialogue, verdicts and other story elements from the selected characters, circumstances and, where applicable, that date's selected comments. Some examples and scenes are prewritten. The characters are not real people talking to you, and their apparent feelings are part of the fiction.

The AI may invent facts or reproduce information you put into a prompt. A MATCH or REJECT is a game outcome, not an assessment of a real person's relationships, emotions or suitability. The game never analyses your face, voice or any biometric data and does not try to infer your emotions; it only reads the text you type, and mood labels on characters are part of the script. Contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to report misleading content or request human review of a moderation decision.

## Moderation

Before a transcript is published, an automated word-pattern filter checks it for sexual content involving minors, private contact details (emails, phone numbers, street addresses) and direct threats. A match hides the transcript and opens a review item for us. Content reported in the app by another player is also hidden as soon as the first report is received. Both steps are provisional: a person reviews every hidden item and decides whether to restore, keep hidden, delete or restrict the account. No decision that restricts your account is taken automatically. To contest any moderation outcome, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the URL and your reasons.

## How long we keep it

- Account data and associated public game content are kept while the account exists, until deletion or moderation removes them. Accounts with no sign-in for 24 months are deleted, with the same effects as deleting the account yourself; we check for them at least every three months. Local game state remains until you clear it or use account deletion.
- Queued personality fields are removed when the queued date is marked played. Unplayed queue entries older than 7 days are removed by a daily purge, so deletion occurs at the next purge rather than at the exact seventh-day timestamp.
- Detailed hosted usage counters are purged daily once their day is more than 35 days old. Short-lived turn-rate logs are pruned during use and by the daily retention job.
- Reports are kept while under review and ordinarily deleted by a daily purge 90 days after resolution. A specific legal need may justify a documented extension with an expiry date.
- Provider logs: {{DB_PROVIDER}} keeps authentication and function logs for the short period set by our current plan (between one and seven days); {{HOSTING_PROVIDER}} keeps no access logs for this site on the current plan. Backups made by providers expire under their own schedules. Deleting live application data does not instantly remove every backup or a copy independently made by another person.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Profile (Delete account). This removes the account and its associated game profile, owned dates, pending dates, comments and reactions. If you delete a comment that was quoted in a published date, that date is taken down too, and a date still being generated will not publish it. Comments that were not quoted are removed on their own. The characters and queue entry of the date owner remain theirs.

Usage counters and their account identifier remain until the daily purge described above to reconcile requests already in progress, prevent abuse and enforce the spending cap. They do not contain the conversation text. Reports can remain for review, without the deleted account link; text identifying you is assessed when handling a rights request. Older publications made before signing in are tied to the device and are not deleted with it: send their URLs to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Dates published before 17 September 2026 may need us to check them by hand; email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the URL.
